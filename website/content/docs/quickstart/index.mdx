## Honesty rules these pages follow

Earlier versions of these docs had three recurring problems: a broken package import, an over-claim of maturity, and conflating cryptographic integrity with regulatory conformity. These pages try not to repeat them.

- Base L2 anchoring defaults to **Base Sepolia testnet**. Mainnet works by setting `BASE_NETWORK=mainnet` (real funds; the EAS schema is registered on your first anchor). Anchoring needs the `[blockchain]` extra and a funded wallet in `EVM_PRIVATE_KEY`.
- DPDP (India) is **referenced but not implemented**. GDPR Article 17 (erasure) is implemented. Anything DPDP-shaped is called out as planned for v0.5.
- LangChain, OpenAI Agents SDK, and CrewAI are **real** integrations: MCP-based examples since v0.3.0, and first-party adapter classes in the package (`attestix.integrations.*`) since v0.4.0. Dify, Google ADK, Semantic Kernel, and Strands are **example-only** (example scripts, not first-party adapters).
- Maturity is **beta with one maintainer**. There is no third-party security audit. The signing key is stored locally by default. Treat artefacts produced today as evidence, not as a substitute for legal counsel or a notified body.
